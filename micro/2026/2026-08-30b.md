People who complain about 60Hz refresh rate screens on iPhones are full of it. I grant you, modern phone screens are really nice. They are bright and the colors are vivid. But I cannot tell a difference in the experience of a 120Hz screen from one with a 60Hz refresh rate in daily use. And my guess is that most regular consumers would not either. Maybe if I had two phones side by side and really looked for the difference I would see it. But otherwise it is spec sheet inflation and a drain on precious battery life. A 60Hz screen on a phone gives you much better battery. And that is a difference you will notice in daily use.
